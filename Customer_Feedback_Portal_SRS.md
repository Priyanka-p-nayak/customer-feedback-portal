# SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION (SRS)
## Customer Feedback Portal

### 1. Purpose
The purpose of the Customer Feedback Portal is to provide a simple platform where customers can submit feedback about products or services. Staff members can view the submitted feedback and use it to improve customer satisfaction.

### 2. Scope
The portal will allow customers to submit feedback through an online form. Staff members will be able to log in, view feedback, search and filter feedback, and update its status. The system will help manage customer feedback in one place.

### 3. Users
1. **Customer:** Submits feedback about products or services.
2. **Staff Member:** Views, searches, filters, and manages submitted feedback.
3. **Administrator:** Manages staff access and system settings.

### 4. Functional Requirements
1. **Submit Feedback:** The system shall allow customers to submit feedback using an online form.
2. **Validate Form:** The system shall check that required fields are completed before submission.
3. **Confirmation Message:** The system shall display a confirmation message after successful submission.
4. **Staff Login:** The system shall allow authorized staff members to log in securely.
5. **View Feedback:** The system shall allow staff members to view feedback submitted by customers.
6. **Search and Filter:** The system shall allow staff members to search and filter feedback.
7. **Update Status:** The system shall allow staff members to update feedback status, such as New, In Progress, or Resolved.
8. **Manage Staff Access:** The administrator shall be able to manage staff access to the portal.

### 5. Non-Functional Requirements
1. **Speed:** The portal should display pages and submit feedback within 3 seconds under normal operating conditions.
2. **Security:** The system shall protect customer information and restrict staff functions to authorized users.
3. **Accessibility:** The portal should support keyboard navigation, readable text, sufficient colour contrast, and clear form labels.
4. **Reliability:** The system should store submitted feedback correctly and avoid losing records during normal operation.

### 6. Assumptions
1. Customers have access to the internet and a web browser.
2. Customers can submit feedback without creating an account.
3. Staff members have authorized login credentials.
4. An internet connection is available to use the portal.
5. The organization will provide the necessary hosting and database facilities.

### Conclusion
The SRS defines the purpose, scope, users, and requirements of the Customer Feedback Portal. It will guide the team while planning and developing the project.
